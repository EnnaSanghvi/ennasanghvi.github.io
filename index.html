Studying the paradox of survival.

Humans are built to survive. Yet some of us, at times, want to hurt ourselves or not exist. I'm Enna Sanghvi, and my research asks how that wish rises and falls, for whom, and what could help in the moment it matters, especially among trauma-exposed and vulnerable populations.

Read my story See the research

Risk isn't a fixed trait. It moves. My work tries to see the shape of that movement.

Three threads, one question

Everything I've done connects to these.

01

Dynamics

How emotion regulation and self-injurious thoughts shift moment to moment, measured with ecological momentary assessment.

 02

Heterogeneity

Why the same diagnosis or exposure looks different from person to person, across cultures, groups, and trajectories.

 03

Context & care

Trauma-exposed and vulnerable populations, and the clinical settings where this science has to actually help.
